Synthetic Data Augmentation in Political Text Analysis

  • Synthetic Data
  • Political Text
  • LLM
Ongoing
Advisor: Dr. Samira Zaroudi · QMSS, Columbia University

This thesis examines whether synthetic training text generated by large language models improves detection of underrepresented categories across political text tasks, and whether any predictive gains preserve category prevalence and party-level or temporal contrasts. It evaluates classification performance and downstream measurement validity as distinct outcomes.

Climate Change Health-Risk Perception and Psychological Distress

  • Climate Health
  • Psychological Distress
  • PHQ-4
  • HINTS
  • Ordinal Regression
Ongoing
  • Conducting faculty-supervised research using pooled HINTS 6 and HINTS 7 data (N = 12,716) to examine the relationship between perceived health harm from climate change and psychological distress in a nationally representative U.S. adult sample.
  • Constructed a four-level PHQ-4 psychological-distress outcome and estimated multivariable ordinal logistic regression models with marginal effects, adjusting for general health, age, sex, race/ethnicity, education, household income, and survey cycle.
  • Evaluated how climate-related health-risk perception translated into differences in predicted probabilities of good mental health and mild, moderate, or severe psychological distress.
  • Found a graded association: relative to respondents reporting “a lot” of perceived harm, those reporting “not at all” had a 14.5-percentage-point higher probability of good mental health and a 3.5-point lower probability of severe distress.

Sociodemographic Disparities in eHealth Information Seeking

  • eHealth
  • HINTS
  • Health Disparities
Completed
  • Harmonized HINTS 2018, 2020, and 2022 data (N = 9,691) and constructed a composite measure of online health-information seeking, provider communication, and medical-test access; estimated pooled and generation-stratified survey-weighted logistic regressions in SAS, using final sample weights and 150 jackknife replicate weights.
  • Found that eHealth use increased from 78.9% in 2018 to 91.3% in 2022, with particularly strong gains among older cohorts, while disparities associated with sex, education, and household income persisted across Millennials, Generation X, Baby Boomers, and the Silent Generation.

High-Frequency Order Book Direction Prediction

  • Order Book
  • Price Direction
  • Gradient Boosting
  • Finance
Completed
  • Processed nearly 100,000 Resset limit-order-book records to predict 3-second-ahead mid-price direction; engineered liquidity, order-imbalance, price-pressure, and short-term order-flow features from five-level bid–ask data, using chronological splitting and rolling walk-forward validation to prevent look-ahead bias.
  • Compared linear, kernel-based, and ensemble classifiers; HistGradientBoosting achieved 81.7% test accuracy, 0.815 F1-score, and 0.912 ROC-AUC, with comparable rolling out-of-sample performance. Feature-importance analysis identified level-1 order imbalance as the strongest short-horizon predictor.
Nov 2025 – Jan 2026

Short-Term Appliance Energy Forecasting with Chronos-2 and Gradient Boosting

  • Time Series
  • Chronos-2
  • Gradient Boosting
Completed
  • Built a 60-minute-ahead appliance-energy forecasting pipeline using 10-minute residential data over 30-day (4,321 observations) and 90-day (12,961 observations) windows; compared univariate and multivariate Chronos-2 with Gradient Boosting using causal walk-forward evaluation and time-ordered train–validation splits.
  • On the 90-day window, Chronos-2 achieved approximately MAE = 27.5 and RMSE = 68.0, versus 103.0 and 130.3 for Gradient Boosting. Longer history improved Chronos-2 but weakened the baseline; similar univariate and multivariate results suggested recent target history carried most of the short-horizon predictive signal.
Oct 2025 – Jan 2026

Financial NLP for MD&A Sentiment Analysis and Firm Performance Prediction

  • Financial NLP
  • Sentiment Analysis
  • LLM
  • Firm Performance
  • A-share Market
Completed
  • Built a DeepSeek-based financial NLP pipeline linking 2022 MD&A disclosures from 500 A-share firms with 2023 CSMAR revenue growth; used low-temperature API inference with zero-shot, few-shot, and chain-of-thought prompting to generate 0–10 sentiment scores, benchmarked against human ratings.
  • Few-shot prompting agreed most closely with human ratings (Pearson r = 0.78, MSE = 1.23, 85% within one point), while CoT was noisier. Few-shot sentiment also showed the strongest, though modest, revenue-growth relationship (R² = 0.020, p = 0.0016); its negative coefficient suggested optimistic disclosures may act as a contrarian signal.

MBTI Personality Prediction with ML and LLM Fine-Tuning

  • Personality
  • Qwen3
  • LoRA
  • Multi-label Classification
Completed
  • Built a personality-inference pipeline for 8,328 Twitter users, removing explicit MBTI references to reduce label leakage; developed TF-IDF and auxiliary linguistic-feature baselines for 16-type classification, then reformulated the imbalanced task into IE, NS, TF, and JP dimensions and fine-tuned Qwen3-4B-Base with LoRA.
  • Benchmarked multi-label fine-tuning against two zero-training prompting baselines; the fine-tuned model achieved 62.6% mean accuracy and 0.650 Macro F1, outperforming both baselines, with its strongest performance on the NS dimension (F1 = 0.825).
